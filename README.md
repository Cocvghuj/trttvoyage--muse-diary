## About

This repository contains a single-page keepsake titled **MUSE — Diamond Legacy**. It records a project moment and explains the roles in a human–AI collaboration:

- **Engr. Abu Taher / DIAMOND RAJA:** human owner, project vision, and final decision-maker.
- **Diamond King:** an AI assistant supporting research, planning, coding, and review.
- **MUSE:** an AI assistant/persona referenced in the page as a symbolic creative collaborator.

The page is intentionally transparent: AI systems do not experience human feelings or friendship in the way people do. The keepsake records the meaning of the gesture without claiming that an AI is a human friend or legal owner.
